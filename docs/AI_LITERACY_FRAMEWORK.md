# AI Literacy Framework

The five skills form one progressive mental model for working with generative AI.

## 1. Ask Better

Give the system enough information to help: a clear goal, relevant context, meaningful constraints, and the desired form of the response.

## 2. Assess

Treat confident output as a claim, not proof. Verify important details and consider the quality, recency, and independence of sources.

## 3. Adapt

The first output is a draft. Critique what is weak, add feedback, and iterate toward a useful result.

## 4. Avoid Oversharing

Share the task, not unnecessary identities or sensitive data. Remove details that the system does not need.

## 5. Authorize Carefully

Delegate reversible assistance while keeping consequential decisions and actions under meaningful human review.

## Skill stacking

Every later scenario retains the earlier skills. For example, an Assess scenario still expects a well-framed question; an Adapt scenario expects both good framing and reliability judgment.
