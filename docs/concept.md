# Around the A — Game Concept

Around the A is a mobile-first, original pixel-art learning game for young Atlanta adults. Players travel through recognizable Atlanta environments, help local characters, and learn practical AI literacy through consequences and revision rather than quizzes.

## Core loop

1. Arrive at an Atlanta stop.
2. Encounter a realistic problem.
3. Try an action or question.
4. See the consequence and targeted feedback.
5. Revise using the literacy skill in play.
6. Earn XP, A Points, and a passport badge.
7. Unlock the next stop and return to the map.

The first playable stop is Five Points. Ponce City Market unlocks after the player demonstrates at least three of four prompting fundamentals, including goal, context, and constraints.

## Experience principles

- Atlanta is the setting and the connective tissue, not a cosmetic label.
- Learning happens through play: attempt, consequence, revision, progress.
- Player inputs are evaluated for concepts rather than exact phrases.
- Scenarios are data, and the game engine owns progression.
- All visual language is original and uses no commercial game assets.

## Future route branches

After the core AI literacy journey, the map can branch into Career, Creator, Business, and Everyday Life routes. These are intentionally locked placeholders in the first release.
