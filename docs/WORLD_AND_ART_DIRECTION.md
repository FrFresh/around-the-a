# Around the A — WORLD AND ART DIRECTION

> This document supersedes the previous creative art-direction guide and is the creative source of truth.

## Creative North Star

Around the A should feel like an Atlanta game cartridge someone discovered from 1992 whose subject matter comes from the future.

Atlanta gives the world its identity. Gameplay gives the player momentum. AI literacy gives the journey purpose.

## World and Learning Philosophy

Atlanta is not decoration. Atlanta is the curriculum. Build lived experience through MARTA commutes, libraries, coffee shops, HBCUs, parks, murals, festivals, apartments, small businesses, and creative meetups—not tourism.

Learning follows this loop:

> Encounter problem → Make decision → Receive feedback → Try again → Learn → Progress

Never stop gameplay for long educational text.

## NPC and Exploration Philosophy

NPCs teach through conversation. Use Atlanta librarians, students, MARTA staff, entrepreneurs, artists, neighbors, and community organizers. Reward curiosity, exploration, conversation, experimentation, and observation.

## A-Card

The AI Literacy A-Card is inspired by an Atlanta transit card and functions as the player's passport, skill tracker, progression system, and identity.

## Camera and Audio

- Side scrolling
- Parallax
- Dialogue close-ups
- Pixel transitions
- Pixel chiptunes
- Neighborhood and transit ambience
- Minimal UI sounds

## Rendering Rules

- Pixel-perfect integer scaling
- Nearest-neighbor rendering
- No anti-aliasing
- Limited palettes
- Modular tiles

## Design Laws

Never use quizzes as the primary mechanic, reward memorization, interrupt gameplay with lectures, make Atlanta feel like tourism, or allow SaaS UI.

Always teach through interaction, reward curiosity, celebrate experimentation, and keep AI positioned as a tool.

## Visual QA

Every screen must answer yes:

- Does this feel like Atlanta?
- Does the mechanic teach?
- Is exploration rewarded?
- Does it feel like an 8-bit game?
