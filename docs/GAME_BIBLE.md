# Around the A — GAME BIBLE

## Vision

Around the A is an educational adventure game that teaches practical AI literacy through authentic Atlanta experiences, helping curious beginners become confident AI users one neighborhood at a time.

## Mission

Help 18–30-year-olds who are curious about AI but intimidated by it become confident, thoughtful AI users through play.

## Audience

- Ages 18–30
- AI curious but intimidated
- Learns best through interactive experiences
- Wants practical skills for work, school, creativity, and life

## Player Transformation

Before:

- Unsure how to use AI
- Doesn't know what to ask
- Doesn't know when to trust AI

After:

- Asks better questions
- Verifies AI responses
- Iterates effectively
- Protects privacy
- Keeps humans in control

## Product Philosophy

- Educational game first
- Learn by doing
- Atlanta is part of the gameplay
- Every mechanic teaches AI literacy
- Better decisions unlock progress

## Success

Players earn their AI Literacy A-Card by mastering the core literacy skills through gameplay.

## Session Length

- 5–8 minutes per neighborhood
- 30–45 minutes for the complete literacy journey

## Core Principles

1. Learn by doing.
2. Curiosity over perfection.
3. Better decisions over memorization.
4. Atlanta is the world, not the wallpaper.
5. AI is a tool, not a replacement for human judgment.
